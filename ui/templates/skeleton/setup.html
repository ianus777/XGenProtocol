Welcome to XGen

Three steps to create your local identity. Nothing is sent over the network.

  1. Display name
  2. Passphrase
  3. Generate keypair

Choose a display name

This is the name other people will see. You can change it any time.

Protect your identity

Your private key is encrypted with this passphrase and stored locally. If you forget it, your identity cannot be recovered — XGen has no server-side reset.

i

Local only. The passphrase never leaves this machine. Argon2id derives a key, ChaCha20-Poly1305 encrypts xgen-client_keypair.enc.

Generate your keypair

Pressing Create identity generates an Ed25519 keypair on this machine. This is the moment your identity comes into being. No server is contacted.

Display name
Joe
Algorithm

i

What happens next. After your identity exists, XGen will silently scan ws://127.0.0.1:8080/xgen for a local Node and connect if found. If nothing answers, the client reaches READY (unconnected) — no error, no notification.
