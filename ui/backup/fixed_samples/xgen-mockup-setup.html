Choose a display name

This is the name other people will see. You can change it any time from your Identity Profile.

Protect your identity

Your private key is encrypted with this passphrase and stored only on this machine. XGen has no server-side reset — if you forget it, your identity cannot be recovered.

Local only — never transmitted Argon2id derives a key from your passphrase. ChaCha20-Poly1305 encrypts xgen-client_keypair.enc on disk. The passphrase itself is never stored.

Generate your keypair

This is the moment your identity comes into being. An Ed25519 keypair is generated on this machine. No server is contacted.

Back up your keypair file Once generated, copy xgen-client_keypair.enc somewhere safe. Loss of both the file and your passphrase means permanent loss of this identity.
What happens after XGen will silently scan ws://127.0.0.1:8080/xgen for a local Node and connect automatically if found. If nothing answers within 2 seconds, the client reaches READY (unconnected) — no error, no notification. You can connect to a Node manually at any time.
